Prefill is lower mostly by design: Gufo checkpoints the conversation before the assistant framing (so the next turn reuses it), which runs the prompt's last few tokens as a separate pass (about 30 ms on an 8B model). The embed library alone prefills at loom-serve's speed (Qwen3-8B: about 1,750 tok/s vs 1,658).
